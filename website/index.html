How a request is assessed

Four stages. You will always know which one your request is in, who holds it, and what happens next.

Submit

You add items, quantities and clinical justification. A reference number is issued the moment you submit.

You · same day

Review

A named procurement officer checks budget authority, accreditation and whether the item sits on a framework.

Procurement · 3–7 working days

Decision

Approved, or rejected with a written reason. You have 14 days to appeal any rejection.

Procurement · within 14 working days

Fulfilment

A purchase order is raised and the items are delivered. Your department receipts them to close the request.

Supplier · varies by item
