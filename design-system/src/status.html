Request lifecycle

Seven states. Every request is in exactly one of them at any moment, and the same badge renders identically wherever it appears — queue row, detail header, email, printed summary.

StateShapeMeaningWho acts next
DraftSquareStarted, not submitted. Visible only to the requester.Requester
SubmittedCircleReceived and queued. Reference number issued.Procurement
In reviewDiamondA named officer is assessing it.Procurement
Action requiredTriangleReturned with questions. The clock is paused.Requester
ApprovedCircleAuthorised. Proceeding to purchase order.Procurement
RejectedBarDeclined with a stated reason. Appealable within 14 days.Requester
FulfilledCircle, solidDelivered and receipted by the department.Nobody — closed

The greyscale test. Print this page in black and white. Every state must still be distinguishable. That is why each badge carries a shape and a word, not only a hue — and why a new state needs a new shape before it needs a new colour.

Alerts

Page-level messages announcing something the user did not ask for. Inline field errors belong with their field, never up here — an alert that duplicates a field error just makes the user hunt twice.

Requests close for the fiscal year on 30 November
Submissions after that date will be assessed against the 2027 budget.
Request REQ-2026-004871 submitted
You will receive a decision within 14 working days. Keep the reference number for any enquiry.
This request needs more information
The procurement officer has asked two questions. The 14-day clock is paused until you reply.
Request rejected — budget exhausted for this category
The theatre equipment allocation for 2026 is fully committed. You may appeal within 14 days or resubmit against the 2027 budget.

Every alert states the consequence. Not just what happened, but what it means for this person and what they can do about it. A rejection without a reason and a route to appeal is not an acceptable public-service message.

Dialogs

Reserved for decisions that cannot be undone. If the page could show the information inline, it should — a dialog interrupts, and interruption is a cost.

Reject this request?

REQ-2026-004871 will be returned to Al-Amiri Hospital — Theatres with the reason you have written. The department may appeal within 14 days.

This is sent to the requester and recorded in the audit trail.

The confirming button restates the action. Never Yes or OK — a user who has stopped reading should still be able to tell the buttons apart. Focus moves into the dialog on open, is trapped inside it, and returns to the triggering control on close.

Toasts

Confirm that something completed. A toast must never carry the only copy of important information — the reference number below also appears on the page and in an email, because a toast disappears and cannot be recovered.
